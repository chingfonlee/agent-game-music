# Codex Game Audio Skill: Game Scene Recipes

Use these pre-tested recipes to instantly populate Stage 1 (`Spec Card`) and Stage 3 (`manifest.json`) without trial and error.

---

## Recipe 1: Racing & Cycling Time Trial (Heavy Metal / Motivating)
- **Gameplay Target**: High cadenced pedaling, intense effort, heart rate 160+ bpm.
- **BPM**: `120 BPM` (2.0s per bar, 12 bars = 24.0s loop).
- **Point (Rhythm)**: Tight palm-muted galloping rhythm guitar, double-kick drums, punchy snare on 2 & 4.
- **Line (Lead)**: Soaring melodic twin-lead guitar riff, clear hook in 1000–2500 Hz.
- **Plane (Pads)**: Chugging stereo rhythm walls, stadium air.
- **SFX Pocket (3–6 kHz)**: Notch filtered to $\le 8.0\%$ to allow digital timer beeps and bicycle bell through.
- **Stem Split**: Drums & Bass for gear shift / pause states; Lead Guitars for attack sprint.

## Recipe 2: Cyberpunk Infiltration (Synthwave / Stealth)
- **Gameplay Target**: Cover-to-cover movement, surveillance cameras, hacking tension.
- **BPM**: `100 BPM` (2.4s per bar, 8 bars = 19.2s loop).
- **Point (Rhythm)**: Deep 808 kick, metallic side-stick rimshot, crisp 16th-note analog hi-hats.
- **Line (Lead)**: Dark resonant acid bassline (TB-303 style) and muted plucked synth arpeggio.
- **Plane (Pads)**: Warm dystopian analog polysynth pads, dark filtered noise swells.
- **SFX Pocket (3–6 kHz)**: Notch filtered to $\le 6.0\%$ to preserve hacking terminal UI clicks and footsteps.
- **Stem Split**: Lowpass filter other stems at 1500 Hz when player is hidden or hacking.

## Recipe 3: Dark Fantasy Boss Encounter (Orchestral Hybrid)
- **Gameplay Target**: Massive colossal boss, dodging telegraphs, high fatality stakes.
- **BPM**: `130 BPM` (1.846s per bar, 16 bars = 29.54s loop).
- **Point (Rhythm)**: Heavy taiko drums, orchestral bass drum, anvil strikes, marching snares.
- **Line (Lead)**: Aggressive staccato brass section (horns, trombones, tuba) carrying an ominous chromatic motif.
- **Plane (Pads)**: Shivering tremolo cello/contrabass section, church organ drones.
- **SFX Pocket (3–6 kHz)**: Deep cavity left for sword parry sparks and dodge swoosh SFX.
- **Outro Formula**: Triple brass crescendo, cymbal crash swell, low timpani roll ending in sub-bass impact.

## Recipe 4: 16-Bit Retro Platformer (Chiptune FM Synth)
- **Gameplay Target**: Precision jumping, coin collecting, fast level traversal.
- **BPM**: `140 BPM` (1.714s per bar, 8 bars = 13.71s loop).
- **Point (Rhythm)**: 8-bit noise-channel kick and crisp snare with zero long reverb.
- **Line (Lead)**: Catchy dual-square wave melody with vibrato and rapid 8th-note counterpoint.
- **Plane (Pads)**: Fast pulsing triangle wave bass and FM synth brass stabs.
- **SFX Pocket (3–6 kHz)**: High clearance for jump bleeps and coin collection chimes.

## Recipe 5: Cozy RPG Village / Tavern (Acoustic Folk)
- **Gameplay Target**: Rest area, shopping, dialogue, inventory management.
- **BPM**: `90 BPM` (2.667s per bar, 8 bars = 21.33s loop).
- **Point (Rhythm)**: Gentle bodhrán hand drum, acoustic guitar fingerpicking pulse.
- **Line (Lead)**: Warm wooden penny whistle and acoustic violin playing a lilting Celtic melody.
- **Plane (Pads)**: Soft accordion drone, warm wooden room acoustics.
- **SFX Pocket (3–6 kHz)**: Very relaxed (<5.0%) to keep item purchase and NPC greeting dialog crystal clear.
